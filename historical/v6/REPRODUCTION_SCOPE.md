# Reproduction scope

| Check | Included inputs | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public pytest | First-party implementation and synthetic fixtures | Accounting, numerical/candidate contracts, gold separation, limits and failure handling | Empirical superiority or external validity |
| V6 scalar validator | All 301 visible slots, 361 reference slots, candidate outcome multiplicities, 21 missing external slots | Source hashes, denominators, exact rational gain gaps, FP/FN and payment arithmetic | Hidden pair identity, WDC graph legality, exhaustive private audit reproduction |
| Historical paid replay | Synthetic and pseudonymized DBLP graphs/scores, recorded answers, separate references | Saved decision, query, batch and partition equality within the supplied bundles | Fresh supervision, rerun of the WDC experiment, independent observations |
| Six-figure regeneration | V6 public tables and preserved V5/V4 public tables | Reported chart/data consistency | A new statistical sample or robust runtime ranking |
| Private offline audit | Requires original frozen archival manifests, ordered WDC assets and full saved states | Full searched-pool evaluation and separate realized batch values at audited saved states | All reachable states, larger omitted candidates, a deployable gold-aware strategy |

`historical/v5` preserves its exact public payload and earlier disclosure boundaries. Do not merge runtimes across versions as a controlled speed experiment. V6 diagnostic costs are separate from historical selector costs; nested timers must not be summed. Logical score accesses are not units of elapsed time.

Python dependencies are pinned in `requirements-public.txt`; no data download, fitting, repair acquisition, paid API, or GPU is needed for the advertised finite checks. Full workspace pytest and clean extraction pytest have different scopes and are recorded separately in the private delivery receipts. Tests do not open sealed test data. Complete historical recalculation depends on external frozen archival assets that are intentionally not all public.

The published scalar candidate histogram removes identities. Its multiplicity counts are candidate occurrences inside dependent saved states, not independent opportunities. The state ordinal is only a cross-table join key. The underlying private deduplication uses the complete visible state; the public ordinal alone cannot reconstruct that equality check.
