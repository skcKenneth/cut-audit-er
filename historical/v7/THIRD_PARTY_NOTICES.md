# Sources, rights, and limitations

The root LICENSE covers the existing first-party software only. It does not relicense third-party datasets or papers. No authorship or publication status is added by this release.

* Historical DBLP–ACM replay: order-preserving pseudonymized topology/scores, recorded answers and separate evaluator reference; no original texts, original IDs or reverse map. Attribution, provider links, modifications and CC BY 4.0 terms remain in `historical/v5/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the earlier bundled notices. Provider: https://dbs.uni-leipzig.de/research/projects/benchmark-datasets-for-entity-resolution . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
* Synthetic controls: generated by the included first-party code. V4/V5 controls share H0/H9 trajectories; V7 controls are separate fixed probability laws and are explicitly synthetic.
* WDC: aggregate public evidence only. No raw text, record-level graph/scores, gold mapping or reverse identifiers are redistributed. De-identification alone would not resolve the data-license gap.
* ALASKA Camera: https://github.com/merialdo/research.alaska . The linked provider archive was unavailable; no archive or derived record-level data is included. The repository's software license is not treated as automatic permission to redistribute an unseen data archive. Provider issue: https://github.com/merialdo/research.alaska/issues/2 .
* Research papers are cited in `research/review_v7/LITERATURE_MATRIX.md`; third-party full texts are not redistributed. Native TransClean/GraphCR reproductions remain incomplete; proxy adapters are never relabeled as original implementations.

The main paper and supplementary full text are deliberately excluded from this public repository package. They are delivered privately in the Overleaf and review archives. This archive includes no credentials, environment, caches or sealed test assets.
