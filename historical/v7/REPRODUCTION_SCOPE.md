# V7 reproduction scope

Included: first-party code, independent exact-distribution evaluator, fixed settings, correctness fixtures, scalar evidence, deterministic six-figure programs, bilingual site, and historical synthetic/DBLP saved-answer replay. The finite checks do not run a new method or natural acquisition.

The public validator checks exact control arithmetic and scalar same-pool identities. It cannot revalidate private WDC pairs or reconstruct all V6 candidate pools. The private handoff retains V6 scientific inputs, state audits and evaluator results, with explicit historical external-asset dependencies. It is not public redistribution of WDC data.

`HISTORICAL_V6_MANIFEST.json` records the exact path and SHA-256 for every previous public member. Replaced V6 assets are preserved under `historical/v6`; unchanged code, evidence and `historical/v5` are shared once. Historical V5 replay uses its own engine and source-order semantics.

Known-law controls are constructed examples, not representative observations or external validation. Twenty small-graph worlds are outcomes of three fixed laws, not independent statistical samples. The analytic hundred-edge problem is not enumerated as 2^100 observations.

V6's unavailable ALASKA Camera source left 21 slots unstarted; V5's distinct 21 gate-stopped slots and older failures remain historical. V7 does not retry or substitute a source.

Clean extraction reuses installed dependencies. It is not a fresh OS, remote CI, cloud Overleaf or full historical recomputation. Final receipts record commands, exit codes, output hashes and archive integrity separately from test counts.
