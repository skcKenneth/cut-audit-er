# Reproduction scope (V4)

This is a public, bounded inspection package. It supports running the unchanged first-party engine on constructed correctness fixtures, replaying saved synthetic and DBLP paid answers, rechecking saved aggregate evidence, regenerating six figures, and building the website. Declared checks do not download records, fit models, buy new answers or launch the formal acquisition suites.

The full workspace suite, private review-package tests, public tests and GitHub Actions runs are separate scopes. Public replay accesses the bundled DBLP alias score matrices and separate evaluator references. It never accesses private WDC assets. Figure 1 is illustrative; Figure 5 is synthetic, not natural data. Local extraction reuses installed pinned dependencies and is not a fresh dependency installation or remote CI execution.

All 18 exposed requested-budget runs, nine prospective DBLP slots, and 90 synthetic acquisition slots with 180 offline reference-evaluation slots are retained. Actual completion and paid-cost bounds are in evidence/manifest.json; missing and interrupted outcomes remain missing. These slots and query events are not independent replicates. WDC anonymous event positions cannot independently establish pair identity or reconstruct partitions. Its first-pair-divergence values remain projections of private checks. DBLP and synthetic replay instead include sufficient allowed assets to reconstruct their saved trajectories.

The original acquisition additionally requires the hash-bound archives `CUT_AUDIT_ER_NEW_DOMAIN_AND_PAPER_HANDOFF.zip` (SHA-256 338fde650f51516faabc362562cfb32df138c431e774c1eb0839343689ddabae) and `CUT_AUDIT_ER_CONFERENCE_IMPROVEMENT_HANDOFF.zip` (SHA-256 44dadaafd1dcbb207d9cb463b2f99d46bde1873ddab1712a5b8383b4bfc25810), the recorded environment and methodology, and licensed source assets for earlier matching. They are external, not nested in this repository. The historical loader source is included for inspection, not as a claim those assets are downloadable from here.

Source code is copied unchanged except for public-only reporting/build scripts and the documented adaptation of one test module. `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json` records copied hashes. The private archived-source replay test is replaced in this public subset by a constructed direct-versus-cached BC comparison; it does not replace the historical private validation.

Synthetic replay includes its generator, fixed inputs, saved answers and both offline references. DBLP aliases preserve lexical and node/adjacency iteration order; no original IDs, text or reverse table are included. Replay rejects any unrecorded query, compares complete candidate/action/answer/batch/partition/stop/logical-access traces, and evaluates gold only after selector replay. Replay is not an additional scientific experiment.

No sealed test, manuscript full text, WDC graph/scores/reference, original DBLP IDs/text or private review notes are public. Gold and FN headroom are excluded from selector inputs. All conclusions retain single-run timing and limited generalization boundaries. This round delivers local ZIPs only; V4 remote CI and Pages deployment have not been performed.
