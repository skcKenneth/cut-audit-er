What the evidence does not establish證據尚未確立的事

  • Synthetic mechanism effects are not real-data results. The unused DBLP cohort cannot establish a cross-domain interaction or erase prior benchmark exposure.合成機制效果不是真實資料結果；未使用 DBLP cohort 不能確立跨領域交互作用，也不能消除既有 benchmark 暴露。
  • Gold headroom and exact bounds stay offline. A clairvoyant optimum ignores discovery cost; the method-to-bound gap is not a pure ranking error.Gold headroom 與精確界限維持離線；全知最優值忽略答案發現成本，方法與界限差距不等於純排序錯誤。
  • Singleton is a post-cap action-set ablation, not the best possible single-step method. Repeated singleton queries can eventually complete a multiedge cut.Singleton 是截斷後的動作集合消融，不是最佳單步方法；連續單邊查詢也可以完成多邊割集。
  • Historical timeouts, 42 unrun cells and incomplete native comparisons remain. The mincut proxy is not a complete TransClean reproduction. The sealed test remains closed.保留歷史逾時、42格未運行比較及未完成原生比較；mincut proxy 不是 TransClean 完整重現，封存 test 維持關閉。
