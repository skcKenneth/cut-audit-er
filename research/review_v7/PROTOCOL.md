## Fixed questions and scope

1. Does a positive realized single-batch gap identify expected decision error?
2. Can the existing completion proxy misrank expected gain even with exact pair
   marginals, and which declared approximation explains the constructed example?
3. How many complete scores did certification avoid within the *same* saved pool,
   without equating operation counts with total runtime?

No new selector, natural acquisition, data source, fitting, seed search, depth/cap
change, timing repetition, deployment or submission is permitted. FP+FN remains
the original endpoint criterion. The controls additionally use the already
declared fixed-batch gain divided by full query cost; this is not a new primary
natural endpoint or a full-horizon optimal policy claim.

## Four constructive initial-state controls

The exact ordered cases and rational constants are in
`configs/review_v7_controls.json`: independent matching100 at B1; path(.30,.30,.30)
at B1; path(.35,.30,.35) at B1; two three-node cliques with two connector edges plus
a separate pair at B2, both latent separation probabilities1/4.

The matching law is evaluated analytically, not by enumerating2^100 worlds. The
other laws enumerate8+8+4=20 legal reference partitions exactly, with rational
weights. These are purpose-built mechanism controls, not random samples,
independent observations, natural errors or evidence of cross-benchmark validity.
The clique graph is externally specified, not claimed to be the graph obtained
by thresholding every .75 cross-block score. No record text matcher is run.

The visible generator accepts only the declared prior specification and returns
exact pair marginals, graph order and budget. It cannot accept a realized gold,
headroom or evaluator result. A first process invokes the unchanged Full selector
once at each initial state, independently checks its legal pool by node
bipartitions, and exhaustively scores that pool with the frozen score function.
No answer is purchased and no repair trajectory is acquired.

After visible outputs are hashed, a separate process loads the known reference
laws. It independently recomputes partition loss for every complete fixed batch,
counting positive and ineffective answers. It does not invoke a selector or
change the visible evidence. Expected values, expected hindsight gaps, perfect
information values and the four formula substitutions are exact fractions.
No-op has value0 and cost0 by explicit convention. A zero-probability conditioning
event is undefined and must be marked, not silently divided by zero.

The four substitutions are explanatory calculations under a known law: original
proxy, exact all-negative probability only, conditional cross-pair expectation
only, both exact. They are not four deployable methods or natural-data ablations.
Separate exact-real and binary64 values; don't certify true gain with a proxy UB.

## Numerical and statistical interpretation

Conditional on the complete visible history and a fixed pool including the actual
action and no-op, expected hindsight gap equals perfect-information value plus
expected single-batch decision gap. This elementary identity is not new theory.
The additional search term after inserting the proxy winner is signed; it is zero
when a valid positive selection certificate gives the same winner. Fallback is
not covered by that positive-certificate statement.

Natural graphs do not supply the true conditional probability law. Do not compute
the expected decomposition for them from one reference, infer calibration failure,
sum gaps across states, or attribute211natural gaps to a constructed mechanism.
